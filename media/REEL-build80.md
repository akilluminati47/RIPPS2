# Build 80 reel: shot list

For the local reel script, so build 80's reel keeps the build 66 reel's pace and look (same capture, music
bed and title cards). The order follows the release notes; each beat is a few seconds.

1. **Cold open**: the dead-elf mark fades up on black (`assets/ripps2-deadelf.png`), then the boot swoop.
2. **Storage on RIPPS2's own look**: the source name **pops in** letter by letter (Colors and More > Source Name > Pop In), scroll, open an info page, tilt it with the right stick.
3. **Hold SELECT**: step through the themes, one step per hold: RIPPS2 > Adapt > RIPgrid.
4. **Adapt**: frosted art, the case and its reflection, the list on clear glass; info page with 4:3 screenshots tipping as one sheet.
5. **RIPgrid**: D-pad through the 4x2 grid, the breathing glow, a no-cover game's glass tile, tilt the chosen cover, **Circle** to change source.
6. **Colors and More**: on the Color Theme row, step **RIPPS2 > Ember > Blood > Ectoplasm > Amethyst > Bone** with the towers in view, a beat each, then **back to RIPPS2's blue** before leaving the page.
7. **START > STATUS**: Neutrino, POPSTARTER, Ember, BIBLE; install one to USB.
8. **Settings > Achievements > YOUR ACCOUNT**: START in, scroll the list and the detail card, out.
9. **RIPFLOW** a few seconds, **Memory Files** orbs, **Launch Disc** with an audio CD's spectrum.
10. **End card**: the mark, "Kill it: build 80", the download and Discord links.

Captures at 480p unless a beat is about HD; 16:9 for Adapt's widecrop beat.
