# Build 82: HD text that fits, RIPgrid cases that turn over, pages that slide, and the theme pack caught up.

## HD text
- **720p and 1080i** text is the right size again. It came out four times too wide at 720p (two and a quarter at 1080i) and spilled everywhere: thanks for the report.

## RIPgrid
- **Hold the right stick to one side for 2 seconds**: the chosen case flips over to its back cover. It stays turned when you move on and come back; hold again to turn it home.
- **Pages slide**: Down past the second row and the next page slides in, like RIPFLOW's carousel.

## The theme pack for build 82
- **Download the [theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest) again**: the build 81 changes only reach you now (Adapt's case front then back, the disc under LAUNCH DISC, RIPgrid in RIPPS2 Sleek). Make your own: [RIPPS2-themes](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes), every key in one guide.
