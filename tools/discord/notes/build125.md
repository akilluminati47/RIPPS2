# Build 125: the video player gets its buttons, Memory Files opens .tar files as folders, and covers come in HD.

## Get it
- [Download the latest RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2.elf), [RIPPS2-languages.zip](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-languages.zip) and the [theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-themes.zip), edition 122

## New
- **The video player**: Cross pauses, Circle stops, START loops, L1 / R1 jump 10 s, L2 / R2 set the volume in steps of 5. The hints show on any button and fade; paused, they stay.
- **Memory Files**: the open folder is the first row (how many, how many marked, the size, the free space); Cross on it marks them all. INVERT MARKS and SIZE AND FREE SPACE are in ACTIONS.
- **A .tar is a blue folder**: open it, paste art in (big pictures shrunk, BMPs made PNGs), copy it out, delete from it.
- **Cover Art Download in HD**: 280 x 400, or Standard as before.
- **Recent games' info shows at once** when you come back to them.
- **RIPFLOW's info page** keeps the source with RUN and BACK beside it; **OPL themes** hide the sidebar until Circle; the default theme's disc **spins** again.

## Test list
- **A video**: pause, play, L1 / R1, START to loop to the end and round again: does the volume stay where you set it?
- **The volume**: hold L2 down to 0 and R2 back up: smooth, no clicks, the number readable?
- **The hints**: none as the video starts, up on a button, gone a moment later while it plays, kept while paused?
- **A long video over USB or MMCE**: do 10 s jumps land quickly, without a black flash or an old picture?
- **The folder row**: Cross marks all and again none; are the counts and the free space right on each drive?
- **SIZE AND FREE SPACE** on a big folder: the right size, and how long?
- **Open your art.tar**: does it list everything? Paste a few PNGs and a JPG in, then check the art in the list.
- **A big PNG or a BMP** pasted into a .tar: shrunk and sharp, and does the cover show?
- **Delete from a .tar**, then copy a member out to a folder: both right?
- **Cover Art Download in HD**: sharper on your TV, and how long for your library?
- **Back and forth over games** on the info page: is their info there at once the second time?
- **Spin** on the default theme, **Zoom** on RIPFLOW and Adapt, **Pop In** on an older theme?
