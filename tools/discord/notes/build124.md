# Build 124 is RIPPS2 v1.2.4 beta: merge .tar art packs, the Zoom launch disc, and your picture's position kept for each video mode.

## Get it
- [Download the latest RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2.elf), [RIPPS2-languages.zip](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-languages.zip) and the [theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-themes.zip), edition 122

## New
- **Merge .tar art packs**: Cache > ART Pack > MERGE a .tar into the pack, or Memory Files on a .tar. The merged-in pictures win; the kept .tar is swapped in only when whole; then RIPPS2 asks whether to delete the merged-in one.
- **Zoom**, a new launch disc: it pops in, then grows to read clearly and holds. RIPFLOW and Adapt use it; the default theme and RIPGRID spin; older themes pop in.
- **Position for each video mode**: H-POS, V-POS and Overscan are kept per Video Mode.

## Test list
- **Merge** a small .tar into your pack: are the counts right, does the art show after, and is the merged-in .tar deleted only when you pick DELETE IT?
- **A big merge** (HDD art into an MMCE pack): how long, and does a full drive stop cleanly with the old .tar intact?
- **Merge from Memory Files** on a .tar in any folder: does it find your pack?
- **Zoom** on RIPFLOW and Adapt: does the disc read clearly and stay until the game starts?
- **Spin** on the default theme and RIPGRID, **Pop In** on an older theme: right one for each?
- **Launch Disc set to Off**: no disc on any theme?
- **1080i then 480i**: set a position in each; does each mode keep its own after a reboot?
- **A mode you never set**: does it start where your old position was?
- **Your art pack with music and sounds on**: does the list scroll smoothly with the old ART folder still there?
- **PS1 games** with a pack: does their art still show (it comes from the folders)?
- **The boot sound** with the pillars, from the card copy: still on time?
- **EXIT in a game**: back to your boot loader, never black?
