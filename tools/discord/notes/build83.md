# Build 83: star your games, YOUR ACCOUNT made readable, titles centered on any theme, RIPgrid tidied, and a new reel.

## Star your games
- **Favorite is now Star.** R3 stars a game, and the list it fills is **Starred Games** (the source, its toast, L3 and the Settings rows). Your starred games carry over.

## YOUR ACCOUNT
- Laid out like a RetroAchievements profile: unlocked, games and mastered across the top, each game's count in the list, a card per game. Long titles scroll and messages wrap: nothing cut off.

## Any theme, any font
- List titles sit in the middle of the highlight bar, measured from the theme's own font. Adapt's bar no longer rides high, and your own themes need no adjusting.

## RIPgrid (theme pack for build 83)
- The covers sit a little lower and smaller, so the chosen one clears the category bar, and the hints read in lowercase. Re-download the [theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest).

## Test list
- **R3** on a game: does the hint say Star, and does the game show under **Starred Games**? Are your old favorites still there?
- **Settings > Achievements > YOUR ACCOUNT**: does every title read whole (scrolling when long), with the right counts?
- **Adapt / Adapt Rx**: is the selected title in the middle of the highlight bar? Try your own theme too.
- **RIPgrid** (new theme pack): does the chosen cover clear the category bar? Hints in lowercase?
- Still open from build 82: HD text at 720p and 1080i, the RIPgrid flip (hold the right stick 2 s) and the page slide.
