## Kill it: RIPPS2 build 82 (alpha)

**Judge a game by its back cover.** RIPgrid turns its cases over now, HD text stopped shouting, and the theme pack finally caught up.

**[Download RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/download/v0.0.82-alpha/RIPPS2.elf)**, the **[theme pack for build 82](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest)** (Adapt, Adapt Rx, RIPgrid), and **[theme it yourself](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes)**: every key in one guide, no rebuild.

### New in 82
- **HD text fixed.** At 720p and 1080i the text came out four times (and two and a quarter times) too wide and spilled over every panel, as a tester reported. It is the right size again, the same as 480p. No more big talk.
- **RIPgrid turns cases over.** Hold the right stick to one side for 2 seconds and the chosen case flips to its back cover, leaning the way you hold. It stays turned when you move on and when you come back; hold again to turn it home. A theme picks the back's art with `back_pattern`.
- **RIPgrid turns pages.** The grid moves a whole page at a time, the new page sliding in as the old one leaves, eased like RIPFLOW's carousel.
- **US English on screen**: canceled, color, license, behavior, center. Sorry, colour.
- **The theme pack for build 82**: Adapt's case shows the front, then the back; Adapt Rx keeps its side bar on the left; both keep the disc under LAUNCH DISC; RIPgrid is set in RIPPS2 Sleek. If you downloaded the pack for build 81, download it again: the build 80 pack was still the latest one until now.

### Your sounds
RIPPS2 was voiced with Sony's own PS2 sounds and a Nintendo track to browse by. None of it ships, but **[the sound map](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2#the-sound-ripps2-was-made-with)** shows every event's sound, and **[RIPPS2 Audio Studio](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/tree/main/tools/audio-studio)** rips them from your own console's BIOS into an `AUDIO` folder beside `RIPPS2.elf`.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). Alpha: keep your usual loader close by.
