## Kill it: RIPPS2 build 94 (alpha)

**Hunt it, watch it, theme it.** Six builds since 88: what every game has left to hunt (on YOUR ACCOUNT and in the in-game menu), pictures and video in Memory Files, a launch disc that pops in or spins, your own button hints and two new glyph sets, every console sound ripped, and RIPgrid built in.

**[Download RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/download/v0.0.94-alpha/RIPPS2.elf)**, and the **[theme pack for build 94](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest)** (RIPgrid's disc, serials and title are new).

### New in 94: what is left to hunt (Spirit of RA)
- **CHECK GAME SUPPORT brings the whole set home.** With **OPEN TO THE NETWORK** on in xeRAbora's settings, the check also keeps the game's achievements beside its list (`RA/<serial>.ach`): each one's title, points, how it is earned and whether you have it. With it off, the check is as before.
- **Achievements > YOUR ACCOUNT**: each game's card says how many are left. Cross opens **LEFT TO HUNT**, every locked achievement with its points and how to earn it.
- **In-game menu**: ACHIEVEMENTS says how many are left, and confirm opens LEFT TO HUNT in the pause menu, one at a time (Up and Down for the next, Back to the menu). It shows the list from the last check: check again to bring it up to date.

### New in 93: pictures and video in Memory Files
- **PNG and JPG** open full screen, fitted. R2 and L2 zoom, the D-pad moves, the right stick tilts, Triangle fits it again. Any size opens.
- **AVI video** (XviD or DivX, MP3 or MP2 sound) plays full screen. **START pauses**, Back or SELECT stops, nothing else on the pad is taken. The player is RIPPS2's fork of libsmslite (the SMS player's core).

### New in 91 and 92: the launch disc, Flow and Grid
- **As a game launches, its disc comes in** where Please Wait used to be: **Pop In** (RIPgrid) or **Spin** (RIPFLOW), picked in **Interface > Flow and Grid** (was Coverflow Settings), with **Grid Layout**, and **Vignette Other Covers** in place of Dim.
- **RIPgrid is built in** as `<RIPgrid>`. On the Grid a game with no cover shows its serial, and the chosen name sits under the grid.
- **The category bar** looks the same on every theme; themes can stack it down the side or reword it. **Page Transition**: Fade or Flash between categories.
- Fixed: PS Emulation's **USB BDMA Driver** row ("<no values>"), hidden rows leave no gap, the Network page's layout, and copies of **folders** are called folders.

### New in 90: Hints and Glyphs
- **Controller Settings > Hints and Glyphs**: hint layout (row or column), labels or glyphs only or **Badges**, show or hide each hint, your own **Hint Order**, and the **Glyph Set** on every page: the theme's, RIPPS2's, **Standard** (Adapt's) or **White** (Grunge's).

### New in 89: every console sound
- **BIOS Sounds > Rip** now makes the **boot** and **launch** too, from the console's own system music. **RIPPOPS**: missing `POPS.ELF` and `POPS_IOX.PAK` are copied from a `POPS` folder on another drive. Settings pages get a **scroll bar**, and the right stick slides them.

The in-game menu (Settings > General > In-Game Menu) still needs a real console: PCSX2 cannot show it.
