# Build 94: what every game has left to hunt, pictures and video in Memory Files, a launch disc that pops in or spins, your own button hints, and every console sound ripped.

## Left to hunt (94)
- With xeRAbora's **OPEN TO THE NETWORK** on, CHECK GAME SUPPORT keeps the game's whole set. **YOUR ACCOUNT** says how many are left; Cross opens **LEFT TO HUNT** (points and how to earn each). The **in-game menu**'s ACHIEVEMENTS opens the same list while you play.

## Pictures and video (93)
- **Memory Files** opens PNG and JPG full screen (R2/L2 zoom, D-pad moves, right stick tilts) and plays AVI video (XviD or DivX): **START pauses**, Back or SELECT stops.

## Launch disc, Flow and Grid (91, 92)
- A game's disc **pops in** (RIPgrid) or **spins** (RIPFLOW) as it launches. **Interface > Flow and Grid**: Grid Layout, Launch Disc, Vignette Other Covers. RIPgrid built in. Page Transition: Fade or Flash.

## Hints and Glyphs (90)
- **Controller Settings > Hints and Glyphs**: row or column, Badges, show or hide each hint, your own order, and the glyph set: Theme, RIPPS2, Standard or White.

## Sounds and PS1 (89)
- **BIOS Sounds > Rip** also makes boot and launch. Missing POPS files are copied from a `POPS` folder on another drive. Settings pages scroll with the right stick.

## Test list

- **In-Game Menu on**, then L1+L2+R1+R2+SELECT+START in that game: ACHIEVEMENTS, then Cross. Do the achievements show? Back to the menu, then Resume: does the game carry on cleanly?
- With the In-Game Menu on, does any game fail to start? Turn it off, try again, and name the game.
- **Memory Files**: open a big PNG and a JPG photo. Fitted? Zoom, move, tilt?
- Play an **AVI** (see the changelog for the ffmpeg line): START to pause and resume, Back to stop. Sound in step? Back to the menu cleanly, music back?
- **Launch a game** on RIPgrid and on RIPFLOW: does the disc pop in or spin, and does the game start as before?
- **Flow and Grid**: try each Grid Layout and Launch Disc setting.
- **Hints and Glyphs**: Badges, Column, your own Hint Order, each Glyph Set. Anything cut off or overlapping?
- **BIOS Sounds > Rip**: boot and launch made too? Do they sound like your console?
- **PS Emulation** with VCD BDMA Apply on Launch: USB BDMA Driver right under it, no gap?
