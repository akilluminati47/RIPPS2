# Build 80 and the first theme pack: Adapt, Adapt Rx and RIPgrid, a STATUS page for RIPPS2's homebrew, and your RetroAchievements in one list.

## The theme pack (RIPPS2-themes)
- **Adapt / Adapt Rx**: each game's art under frosted glass, the cover in RIPPS2's case with its reflection, the list on clear glass. The info page tips with the right stick as one sheet, with 4:3 screenshots.
- **RIPgrid**: your games as a 4x2 cover grid. The chosen cover breathes and tips with the stick; Circle cycles sources.
- **Hold SELECT 4.2 s** on the game list to step to the next theme. A quick press still refreshes.

## STATUS (START menu)
- Neutrino, POPSTARTER, Ember and BIBLE: where each is installed, install RIPPS2's copy, and its settings, under one name each.

## Achievements (now in Settings, after Audio)
- **YOUR ACCOUNT**: every game you have checked, with what your RA account has unlocked. START opens it.
- Badges stay on: they do nothing until TELEMETRY is on and xeRAbora answers on your PC.

## Little things
- **Source Name: Pop In** (Colors and More): ALL GAMES rises in letter by letter. The new themes do it by default.
- Network settings: RIPPS2's rows under their own header, room before OK. Colors and More: Source Name under the glyphs, Color Theme over the pickers.

## Test list
- **Install the themes** (copy the thm_ folders into THM), then **hold SELECT** on the game list: does it step themes once per hold, and does a quick press still refresh?
- **Adapt on 16:9 and 4:3**: art zoomed not stretched? Case and reflection right? Info page tilts as one with the right stick?
- **RIPgrid**: D-pad through the grid, **Circle** to change source, games without covers show their name? Only the chosen cover tilts?
- **START > STATUS**: does it find your Neutrino / POPSTARTER / Ember? Try installing one to USB.
- **Settings > Achievements > CHECK GAME SUPPORT** on a game (xeRAbora running), then **YOUR ACCOUNT**: does the game show with your unlocks?
- **Colors and More > Source Name > Pop In** on the built-in themes.
