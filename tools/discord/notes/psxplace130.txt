[LIST]
[*][B]Every video opens faster[/B], over MMCE most of all: the index is read from the player's buffer, not 4 bytes at a time from the drive.
[*][B]VIDEO.TXT[/B] beside RIPPS2 lists each step of opening a video, so one that never starts says where it stopped. A video RIPPS2 cannot play (a camera's MJPEG) says so and goes back to the list.
[*][B]PS1 games named by a code show their name[/B], read from conf_apps.cfg on the drive the POPS folder is on, on the list and the info page.
[*][B]START rows[/B]: a drive on Manual with nothing saved gets a START row in All Games. Cross starts the drive and its games take the row's place.
[*][B]Memory Files[/B]: a file copied onto a memory card stays, and a full 64 MB card lists in one read.
[*]Every new line translated in all 32 languages.
