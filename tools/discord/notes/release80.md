## Kill it: RIPPS2 build 80 (alpha)

**[Download RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/download/v0.0.80-alpha/RIPPS2.elf)**, plus the first theme pack: **[RIPPS2-themes](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest)** (Adapt, Adapt Rx, RIPgrid).

### New in 80
- **STATUS** on the START menu: Neutrino 1.8.0, POPSTARTER, Ember and BIBLE, each with where it is installed, a way to install RIPPS2's copy, and its settings.
- **Achievements moved to Settings** (after Audio), with **YOUR ACCOUNT**: every game you have checked, from every drive, with what your RetroAchievements account has unlocked. Badges stay on and cost nothing until TELEMETRY is on and xeRAbora answers.
- **Themes**: frosted glass over each game's art, a cover **Grid**, glass tiles with the name for games without a cover, a breathing selection glow, one-sheet right-stick tilt. **Hold SELECT 4.2 s** to step to the next theme.
- **Coverflow and Grid**: Circle cycles the sources (the D-pad is all games there); the hint row says Source.
- **Source Name: Pop In** (Colors and More): the source's name rises in letter by letter. Source Name sits under the glyphs; the Color Theme heads the colour pickers.
- **Network settings**: RIPPS2's rows under their own header, with room before OK.

### Since the build 66 reel (70 to 80)
- RIPFLOW and Colors and More (six colour themes, glyphs, fades), L1/R1 across the tabs, Left/Right across drives.
- Neutrino, POPSTARTER and Ember carried inside the one `.elf`; BIBLE (PS2BBL 1.2.0) on a memory card or the internal HDD.
- Cover Art fetched by the PS2 over HTTPS; 720p and 1080i in one pass at full detail; AUDIO found on any drive.
- Towers and orbs that run all night at a steadier 60 fps; RiptOPL's and RIPPS2's host checks on every build.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). Alpha: keep your usual loader close by.
