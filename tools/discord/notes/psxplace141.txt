The new pages below have only run in the PCSX2 lab, not on a real PS2 yet, so your reports count.

[B]BIBLE's settings, on a proper page[/B] ([URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/media/b141/bible-page.mp4']watch[/URL]). Manage's BIBLE menu now looks like Settings, with a tip on every row saying what it does and what it costs. The cursor stays on its row, and the file is written once when you leave, through a temporary copy read back first, so a pulled card cannot leave BIBLE half a file. Your notes stay.

[B]Launch keys, chosen from your drives.[/B] Each button gets its name and ten paths, chosen with the Memory Files browser, typed, or one of BIBLE's commands, with arguments. BOOT LOCK's lines only show, the last path in the file stays, and a path your card's BIBLE build cannot read says so on its row.

[B]Known:[/B] BIBLE 3282396, the one RIPPS2 carries, can stay on its splash for good when an AUTO path names a file that is not there on a drive it has to load a driver for (USB, MMCE, MX4SIO, HDD). It never reaches the next path. The lab saw this with mmce?:/APPS/RIPPS2.ELF and no device; the other cases come from reading the code. Buttons held at power on are not affected, so a button with a good path gets you past it. For now, give AUTO only paths that are really there on those drives. The fix is written and comes with the next BIBLE, after a re-sign.

[B]Sound fixes[/B] (from 1.4.0; timings and the stray sound report are from a SCPH-39001 with an SD2PSX)
[LIST]
[*][B]Stray sounds:[/B] a ripped sound's end mark can send a voice on to read other memory, which fits another effect playing after one finished. Each sound now ends on its own silent block as it loads, none has been heard since on that console, and nothing needs ripping again.
[*][B]The boot sound comes in with the towers:[/B] 0.26 s after them, down from 4.46 s.
[*][B]The other sounds are ready at once:[/B] 5.12 s with every drive on Auto, down from 42.85 s. A sound folder on a slow drive still waits for that drive.
[*]Drives coming up during the boot stay quiet, and a music start over another start goes no further.
[/LIST]

[B]Set Metadata (Rename)[/B] (from 1.4.0), one name on every row, on a page like Settings, the file name first as it appears in your games list. A tip says what a rename does there. PS2 games find their art and settings by game ID, so nothing changes. PS1 games and Ember titles find theirs by name, so RIPPS2 renames those with the game ([URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/media/b140/rename-ps1.mp4']watch[/URL]). Art inside an ART Pack needs the pack built again.

[B]A timeline for L1 and R1 in the video player[/B] (from 1.4.0, [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/media/b140/scrub.mp4']watch[/URL]). A tap moves 10 s, a hold runs up to 10 minutes a second, and the player jumps once when you let go. Spamming R1 no longer stops and restarts it on every press.

DIAG.TXT now ends with a trace of every sound and who asked for it, so a sound bug can be named from a report. Tell us what breaks here or [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/issues']open an issue[/URL], with your console model, what you did and your DIAG.TXT.
