[CENTER][SIZE=5][B]RIPPS2 v1.2.8 beta (build 128)[/B][/SIZE]

[B]Rip your sounds again.[/B] The PS2 browser plays its menu sounds against 44.1 kHz, and RIPPS2's rips were 8.8% high. Audio settings > BIOS Sounds now rips them at the console's own pitch, measured against the BIOS itself. The menu music was already right.

[LIST]
[*][B]Boot Sound: Off, On, On (fast).[/B] On (fast) keeps the boot sound on the memory card RIPPS2 starts from, so it plays as the pillars rise. STATUS says where it comes from.
[*][B]PS1 art from an ART Pack as fast as PS2 art[/B]: its other names (the disc ID, XX.<name>.ELF) are looked up in the pack, in memory. Build the pack again to get it.
[*][B]The list page's art fades in as one[/B] on every theme; RIPPS2's info page turns the cover to its back with the screenshots; the info pages' stars are RIPFLOW's size.
[*][B]The sidebar's flow[/B]: Circle opens the drawer of the page you are on, Left folds it to the icons, Circle again closes it. Select and Back hints while it has the pad.
[*][B]RIPFLOW[/B]: L2 / R2 step the covers, Up / Down tip the case, the right stick turns it over.
[*][B]CHEATS in the in-game menu[/B] (by NathanNeurotic): turn a game's cheats on and off while you play.
[*][B]PS1 Duplicates[/B] (Ember or POPSTARTER when you have both), [B]conf_apps.cfg names[/B] for POPStarterGameInstaller's numbered games, and [B]POPSTARTER's in-game reset pictures[/B] remade in RIPPS2's look.
[*][B].pad[/B]: RIPPS2's format for any USB pad, one text file each, with a reference reader for any launcher. Settings > Controllers > My Pads checks yours.
[*][B]Starting a drive for a launch[/B] shows an estimate and waits longer, so a Manual HDD works on the first press. Tooltips stay 6.9 s. MMCE shows sizes.
[*][B]RIPPS2-lite.elf[/B]: the same RIPPS2 without the programs it carries for BIBLE and Neutrino.
[/LIST]
Thanks to everyone testing on Discord and GitHub: ahavasi's report shaped half of this one.
