[B]BIBLE for SD2PSX and MemCard PRO 2 owners.[/B] BIBLE's MMCE build now rides in RIPPS2.elf, signed by akilluminati47: with RIPPS2 on the SD card, Settings > General > Boot Loader > Manage installs BIBLE on a genuine memory card in the other slot, and it starts RIPPS2 from the SD card at power on. An SD2PSX or MMCE card itself is never bound (the console refuses it): Manage says so first and offers BOOT LOCK for its own PS2BBL.

[LIST]
[*][B]Slots 0 and 1[/B]: every Manage, BOOT LOCK and STATUS line, and the VMC slots, count them as mc0: and mc1: do.
[*][B]STATUS > RIP THIS CONSOLE'S AUDIO[/B]: opens Audio Settings on BIOS Sounds and BIOS Music.
[*][B]STATUS[/B]: the boot sound line names its file, and says when it is on a memory card.
[*][B]RIPGRID and Adapt[/B]: the source name is Pop In only with the categories across the top (it drew over STORAGE), and over the grid with the sidebar.
[*]Every changed line translated in all 32 languages.
