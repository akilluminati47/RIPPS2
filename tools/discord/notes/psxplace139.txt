[B]BIBLE has its own thread now:[/B] [URL='https://www.psx-place.com/threads/bible.51524/']BIBLE, the boot loader RIPPS2 sets up[/URL], with [URL='https://youtu.be/sTjjmDd6r1g']its own reel[/URL]. Its next update rides inside 1.3.9, signed by akilluminati47, and RIPPS2 sets it up by itself, no KELFBinder needed:

[LIST]
[*][B]Manage's BIBLE menu[/B] (Settings > General > Boot Loader > Manage, with BIBLE on a card): UPDATE BIBLE binds the new one over an older one in place, and Background (your RIPPS2 sky, or black), Splash, Button Glyphs, Info Line, Power-on Wait, Video Mode and Widescreen each change one line of BIBLE's settings file.
[*][B]The boot sound, on the card[/B]: Boot Sound On (fast) keeps boot.adp beside BIBLE's settings on the card, read before any drive starts.
[*][B]A faster start[/B]: DISC_STOP = 0 in BIBLE's settings file stops BIBLE waiting on the disc drive at launch. It took 3.3 s off a PCSX2 run, and a disc in the tray keeps spinning into RIPPS2. The race is in [URL='https://www.psx-place.com/threads/bible.51524/']BIBLE's thread[/URL].
[*][B]Every older BIBLE is recognized[/B] ("BIBLE (MMCE build, older)"), so the update is offered whatever installed it.
[*]Tested on a SCPH-39001 with an SD2PSX: UPDATE BIBLE bound the new BIBLE over the old one, and it booted RIPPS2.
[/LIST]

[B]Set up or update BIBLE wherever you use it[/B]: a genuine memory card (RIPPS2 on the card or USB), an SD2PSX or MemCard PRO 2, MX4SIO, or the internal HDD's boot area (Manage > SET UP, UPDATE OR RESTORE BIBLE). So far only the SD2PSX has run on a real PS2, so the rest are wanted most, on PAL and Japanese consoles too.
