# Build 135: one RIPPS2.elf with BIBLE inside, and Neutrino found where you keep it, never carried.

## Get it
- **RIPPS2.elf**, **RIPPS2-languages.zip** and the **theme pack** (edition 128) are attached below, or from the [release page](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/tag/v1.3.5-beta).
- Put it in place of your old RIPPS2.elf (or RIPPS2-lite.elf); your settings stay where they were.

## Neutrino
- **RIPPS2 no longer carries Neutrino**: it uses yours, from Game Launching > Neutrino's path, a `neutrino` folder on the game's drive or a memory card, or wherever LOOK FOR THEM found one. Get it from [rickgaiser's releases](https://github.com/rickgaiser/neutrino/releases).
- **No Neutrino anywhere**: the game offers to search the drives, then says where to get it and where to put it, then starts on the OPL core.
- **STATUS** shows where Neutrino was found; the install row is gone.

## One ELF
- **RIPPS2-lite.elf is gone.** RIPPS2.elf (2,580 KB) carries only BIBLE now, for Boot Loader > Manage.

## Test list
- **Neutrino on the game's drive** (`neutrino` folder): does a Neutrino game start?
- **Neutrino on a memory card** (`mc0:/neutrino` or `mc1:/neutrino`): does it start from there?
- **A path in Game Launching > Neutrino**: is that one used?
- **No Neutrino anywhere**: does the game offer the search, then say where to put it?
- **STATUS > LOOK FOR THEM**: is your Neutrino found and shown under LAUNCHERS?
- **STATUS**: no INSTALL RIPPS2'S NEUTRINO row, and NEUTRINO SETTINGS says where to get it?
- **APA HDD with an old Neutrino (before 1.3)**: does the message ask for 1.3 or newer?
- **About**: v1.3.5 beta, build 135, Neutrino as the second game core?
- **Were you on the lite ELF?** Does RIPPS2.elf take its place with your settings kept?
- **BIBLE with Manage** on a **spare memory card**: does the console take it?
- **Power on with that card**: does BIBLE start RIPPS2 at once (BOOT LOCK)?
- **Your language**: are the new Neutrino lines translated?

## Report
Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/issues) with your **console model**, **where your Neutrino is** (and its version), and your **DIAG.TXT** (STATUS > SAVE DIAGNOSTICS).
