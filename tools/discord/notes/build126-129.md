# Builds 126 to 129: set up any USB pad on the PS2, the BIOS sounds at the console's own pitch, PS1 art as fast as PS2's, the new sidebar flow, CHEATS in the in-game menu and a lite ELF.

## Get it
- [Download the latest RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2.elf) (or the smaller [RIPPS2-lite.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-lite.elf), without PS2BBL and Neutrino), [RIPPS2-languages.zip](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-languages.zip) and the [theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-themes.zip), edition 128
- **Rip your sounds again** (Audio settings > BIOS Sounds) for the right pitch, and **build your ART Pack again** for the faster PS1 art.

## New
- **Set Up A USB Pad** (Controllers): press each button it asks for and RIPPS2 writes the pad's .pad. It steers the menu and plays in games (Pad Emulator on); PADS/GAMES/<game ID>.pad remaps one game.
- **BIOS sounds at the console's pitch** (the rips were 8.8% high). **Boot Sound On (fast)** plays with the pillars.
- **PS1 art from an ART Pack as fast as PS2 art**; the list page's art fades in as one.
- **The sidebar**: Circle opens it on your page, Left or Circle folds it, Circle closes it. Select and Back hints.
- **CHEATS in the in-game menu** (Ripto's). **RIPFLOW**: L2 / R2 step, Up / Down tip the case.
- **PS1 Duplicates**, **List Title and Description**, conf_apps.cfg names, sizes on MMCE, bigger stars, 6.9 s tooltips.
- **POPSTARTER's reset pictures** in RIPPS2's look (extras/POPS).

## Test list
- **Set Up A USB Pad** with a pad RIPPS2 did not know (copy DS3, 8BitDo, arcade stick): does it find every button and stick?
- **After saving**: does the pad move the menu, and play in a game with the Pad Emulator on?
- **A hat d-pad, analog L2 / R2**: found right? SELECT skips a button you do not have?
- **Your DualShock 3 / 4 and the ShanWan copy**: still fine in the menu and in games?
- **A game map**: `cross=circle` in PADS/GAMES/<the game's ID>.pad swaps them in that game only?
- **Rip the sounds again**: do cursor, confirm and back match the browser's pitch?
- **Boot Sound On (fast)** with RIPPS2 on a memory card: does it start with the pillars?
- **Enter a menu** (START, Triangle, Square): one sound each time?
- **Random game (L2 + R2)** many times: does any beep keep ringing?
- **PS1 art** from an ART Pack built with this build: as quick as PS2 art?
- **Change themes** a few times with art.tar on MMCE: does every page and the cover flip still work?
- **The sidebar**: Circle, Left, Circle on every page and theme: does it open and close where it should?
- **CHEATS in the in-game menu** on a game with a .cht: turn one off and on, RESUME: does it hold?
- **HDD on Manual**: does a launch start on the first Cross?
- **IGR in Fatal Frame 3 (SLUS_212.44)** at the title screen: menu or reset?
- **RIPPS2-lite.elf**: everything the same, and BIBLE / Neutrino installs say they need the full ELF?
- **Send us your .pad** files with the pad's name, so the next person with that pad can drop it in.
